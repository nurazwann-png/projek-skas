Act as an Expert Full-Stack Developer and AI Architect specializing in Next.js (App Router), React, Serverless architectures, and LLM integrations. 

# PROJECT OVERVIEW
I am building an "AI-First" Centralised District Monitoring Web Application for the Ministry of Education Malaysia, specifically for managing the SK@S (School Quality Management System). The app serves as a smart data center where teachers upload achievement certificates, and the AI automatically extracts data, categorizes it based on official SK@S rubrics (using RAG), and generates official descriptions. It also acts as a monitoring dashboard for the District Education Office (PPD).

# TECH STACK
- Frontend & Framework: Next.js 14+ (App Router), React, Tailwind CSS
- UI Components: Shadcn UI, Lucide Icons, Recharts (for dashboards)
- Database: Neon Postgres (Serverless) with `pgvector` extension for RAG
- ORM: Drizzle ORM
- Authentication & RBAC: Clerk (Roles: PPD Admin, School Admin, Teacher)
- AI & LLM: Vercel AI SDK, Claude 3.5 Sonnet (for text generation & RAG reasoning), Claude Vision (for OCR on certificates)
- Deployment: Vercel

# CORE FEATURES & WORKFLOW
1. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):
   - Teacher (Data Entry): Uploads certificates, inputs basic details.
   - School Admin / Principal (Approver): Reviews AI-generated drafts, edits, and approves to finalize the data.
   - PPD Admin (District Monitor): Read-only access to a bird's-eye view analytics dashboard of all schools in the district.

2. AI OCR & Auto-Drafting (Zero Friction Entry):
   - Teacher uploads a certificate image.
   - Claude Vision extracts: Program Name, Date, Level (District/State/National), and Teacher Role.
   - Vercel AI SDK auto-generates official SK@S justification texts based on the extracted data.

3. RAG Validator System:
   - Uses `pgvector` in Neon DB to store embeddings of the official SK@S Manual.
   - Cross-references the uploaded achievement with the SK@S rubric to suggest the correct Standard/Score and auto-tag the entry (e.g., #NationalLevel, #Standard3.2).

4. Dashboards:
   - School Dashboard: Tracks pending approvals and achievement metrics.
   - PPD Dashboard: Aggregated metrics, filtering by school/standard, and identifying schools needing intervention.
